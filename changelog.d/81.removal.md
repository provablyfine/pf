The `ssh-shell`, `ssh-port-forwarding` and `ssh-command` grant types are removed, along with the `pfa grant ssh-shell`, `pfa grant ssh-port` and `pfa grant ssh-command` subcommands. Use `pfa grant ssh` with `--capability`: a former `ssh-shell` grant is `--capability shell pty user-rc`, plus `agent-forwarding` and `x11-forwarding` where those were permitted; a former `ssh-port-forwarding` grant is `--capability port-forwarding`; a former `ssh-command` grant is `--cmd`. Stored grants are converted automatically by the database migration.
